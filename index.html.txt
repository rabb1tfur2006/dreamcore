<p dir="auto"><strong>What I built:</strong></p>
<p dir="auto">A full-screen interactive dreamcore exploration experience with three layers:</p>
<ol>
<li dir="auto">
<p dir="auto"><strong>Homepage</strong> — A pastel dreamcore landscape with rolling green hills, oversized fluffy clouds, and a tiny isolated house. The title "D R E A M A T L A S" fades in slowly, clouds drift imperceptibly, and the entire scene has subtle parallax movement that follows your mouse. A minimal "EXPLORE THE DREAM" button sits quietly below.</p>
</li>
<li dir="auto">
<p dir="auto"><strong>Dream Atlas Map</strong> — A dark spatial map where 7 floating location nodes (Poolrooms, Grassland, Suburb, School, Hotel, Highway, Unknown) float in space with hand-drawn SVG icons. Dotted lines connect related locations. Hovering reveals the location name and a faint subtitle, with gentle parallax based on mouse position.</p>
</li>
<li dir="auto">
<p dir="auto"><strong>Environment Viewer</strong> — Full-screen scenes with drag-to-look parallax, atmospheric color grading, film grain, and 2-3 hidden hotspots per scene. Clicking a hotspot reveals sparse, mysterious text ("This house was not here before."). Certain hotspots trigger rabbit hole connections — after showing a hint like "you found something," you're transported to an unexpected location. The Unknown area is deliberately emptier and stranger than the rest.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Audio system</strong> — Procedurally synthesized ambient soundscapes using the Web Audio API. Each environment has its own chord progression, filter frequencies, and noise levels (e.g., Poolrooms uses low rumbling tones; Grassland uses brighter, airier chords). A tiny "♪ DREAM AUDIO" control sits in the corner, fading in/out smoothly. Audio file paths are defined in the data structure so you can swap in real music later.</p>
<p dir="auto"><strong>Technical notes:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li dir="auto">All environments are data-driven via <code node="[object Object]">src/data/environments.ts</code> — easy to add or modify</li>
<li dir="auto">Image URLs point to Pexels placeholders and can be replaced with your own artwork at the paths specified in the data file</li>
<li dir="auto">Fully responsive with touch-drag support on mobile</li>
<li dir="auto">No login, no database, no backend — purely a self-contained interactive experience</li>
</ul>
